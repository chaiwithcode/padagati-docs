Support

Need a hand with step tracking, Apple Health or reminders? Start with the checks below, then report a problem through the project’s public issue page.

Contact support on GitHub

Issues are public. Describe what happened and the iOS version. Do not post step history, screenshots containing health details, account credentials or other private information.

Steps are missing

  • Carry your iPhone while walking and allow Motion & Fitness access in iPhone Settings under Privacy & Security.
  • Step readings can take a short time to appear.
  • To include Apple Watch or other Health activity, enable Apple Health sync in PadaGati and review the categories shared with the app in Apple Health. Apple does not tell apps which read categories were denied, so missing readings can mean no data or limited access.

Apple Health sync

In PadaGati, open Settings → Apple Health. Turning sync off stops new Health queries from the app; previously imported records remain until you delete local activity data. Turning sync off in PadaGati does not revoke permission in Apple Health.

Walking reminders

Reminders are optional local notifications. Enable them in Settings → Walking Reminders and allow notifications when iOS asks. If permission was denied earlier, change it in iPhone Settings. Reminders inside configured quiet hours are skipped.

Still stuck?

Open a support issue with a short description, the iOS version, and steps to reproduce. Please keep health details private.
