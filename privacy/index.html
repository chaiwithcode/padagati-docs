Privacy policy

PadaGati is designed to keep your walking information on your iPhone.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Information on your device

PadaGati stores activity history, recorded walks, milestones and preferences locally on your device. The app requires no account, uses no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs, performs no location tracking, and does not transmit movement data to external servers. We do not receive or collect your step totals, movement data, or walking history.

The app uses Apple’s Core Motion pedometer for iPhone step and walking measurements. If you choose to enable Apple Health sync, PadaGati can read shared activity measurements, including steps, walking and running distance, flights climbed, wheelchair distance and wheelchair pushes. Health access is optional and read-only: PadaGati does not write activity data to Apple Health.

In accordance with Apple Developer Program Guidelines, information read from Apple Health is never used for marketing, advertising, or use-based data mining, and is never sold, shared, or transferred to advertising platforms, data brokers, or third parties.

Optional developer tips (In-App Purchases)

PadaGati offers optional one-time consumable tip purchases (“A Little Thanks”, “A Warm Thanks”, “A Big Thanks”) solely to support independent development. Tips do not unlock features or restrict app functionality. All purchases are processed securely by Apple via StoreKit and the App Store. PadaGati does not collect, access, or store your payment card numbers, billing details, or financial accounts. Transactions are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy and Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions.

Widgets and Live Activities

If you add a PadaGati widget or use a walking Live Activity, iOS may display selected activity information in that system surface. Widgets read a small snapshot from an on-device shared container. Walking Live Activities receive updates locally through Apple’s ActivityKit. These surfaces do not send your activity data to us.

Notifications

Optional walking reminders are scheduled as local iPhone notifications after you enable them and grant notification permission. Their schedule and preferences stay on your device. Some reminders may include progress recently observed by the app. iOS controls when background readings and notifications are available. You can change or turn off reminders in PadaGati or iPhone Settings.

Sharing a card or sticker

Cards and transparent stickers are rendered on your iPhone from the recorded activity you choose. Opening a preview does not upload it. If you use the iOS share sheet, you choose which app or person receives the exported image; that destination’s privacy practices then apply.

Copy Sticker places the PNG on your local clipboard for up to five minutes. Universal Clipboard sharing is disabled for that clipboard item. Apps you paste into can receive the image. Exported copies may remain with the destination even after you delete PadaGati’s local history.

Data sharing and backups

PadaGati does not sell or share your activity information with us, advertisers, or data brokers. iOS device backups are controlled by Apple and your device backup settings; check those settings to understand whether app data is included in your backups.

Deleting local data

Use Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete Local Activity Data in the app to remove stored daily records, recorded walks, milestones and unfinished walk checkpoints. This also turns off Health sync and reminders. Your goal, unit preference and iOS permissions remain. The action does not delete Apple Health records or revoke Health permission. Recent iPhone motion readings may appear again as tracking continues, and reconnecting Apple Health can import its history again.

Your choices

You can turn off Health sync in PadaGati and manage the categories shared with the app in Apple Health. Motion access is controlled in iPhone Settings under Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness. Notification permission is controlled in iPhone Settings.

This website and support

These documentation pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes under its privacy statement. This site adds no analytics, tracking scripts, cookies or third-party fonts. If you open a GitHub support issue, the information you submit is public and may be read by the developer and others. Do not include personal health information.

Questions

For privacy questions, email support.chaiwithcode@gmail.com. If you contact support, we receive your email address and information you choose to include to answer your request. Support correspondence happens outside the app and is not automatically linked to your local activity records. Please avoid sending unnecessary health details.
