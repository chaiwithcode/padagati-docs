A calmer way to keep moving

Walking, at your pace.

PadaGati is an iPhone walking and step tracker. It brings together daily steps, walking history and timed walks, with optional Apple Health sync.

Your movement stays on your device. PadaGati has no account, advertising, location tracking or movement-data uploads.
